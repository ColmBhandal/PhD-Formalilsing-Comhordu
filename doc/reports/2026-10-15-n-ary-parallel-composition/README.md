# Report: n-ary parallel composition

2026-10-15 · step 16 of the [workstream](../../workstreams/2026-10-13-rocq-upgrade-and-proof-completion.md) · [#6](https://github.com/bhandalflip/PhD-Formalilsing-Comhordu/pull/6)

`parAll p ps` in `src/lang/SoftwareLanguage.v` is the parallel composition of one or more processes, nested to the right as `$||$` parses, so `parAll p1 [p2, p3]` computes to `p1 $||$ p2 $||$ p3` and the protocol's triples are instances of it with no statement changed. The results about it are proved once, by induction on the list: inversion of a delay and of a discrete step, lifting of a component's step and enabledness, no synchronisation between two components while the whole delays, and bisimilarity as a congruence. A step at a position of the list is a `stepAt`, a generic list relation in `src/lib/GenBasics.v`, and a fact about every component is a `Forall2`. The 28 triple-shaped lemmas (`parTriple_*`, `link_par_triple_*`, `del_triple_sort`, `par_del_sync_not` and `par_bisim_triple`) are gone. The protocol reaches its three components through one tactic, `triple_pos` in `ProtAuxResults.v`, and the `link_partrip*` tactics keep their names and the hypotheses they produce, so the long `NAR*` proofs are untouched. `net_del_elim` is the one restated result, now over an entity with no process structure, and `safety` still rests on 84 assumptions. The list of each removed lemma with its replacement is in the PR and in this report's first version, in the git history.

Two things were left for later. `link_reach_triple_tau` stays: it is a one-line instance of `parAll_disc_inv`, but it states the six synchronisation cases in the protocol's shape and four `NAR*` proofs consume exactly that shape. `parStructPres` and `parStructPres2` in `SoftwareLanguage.v` are pair-shaped, unused, and instances of `parAll_disc_weak` and `parAll_del_inv`; the workstream's final phase lists their deletion.
